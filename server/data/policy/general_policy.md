# Loan Policy Guidelines

## Eligibility Criteria
- SMEs must be registered in Sri Lanka for at least 2 years.
- Minimum annual turnover of LKR 1,000,000.
- Business must operate in approved sectors (Agriculture, Manufacturing, IT, etc.).
- Owner must have a clean CRIB record.

## Loan Categories
1. **Working Capital Loan**: For daily operations, inventory, and payroll.
2. **Expansion Loan**: For opening new branches or expanding capacity.
3. **Machinery & Equipment**: For purchasing specialized gear.

## Repayment
- Monthly installments via bank transfer or direct debit.
- Grace period of 3-6 months available for expansion loans.
- Early settlement is allowed with a 1% processing fee.

## Required Documents
- Business Registration Certificate.
- Bank statements for the last 6 months.
- NIC of owner(s).
- Tax returns (if applicable).
