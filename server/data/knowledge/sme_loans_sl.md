## <a id="_Toc220821805"></a>__Commercial Bank of Ceylon – SME Loans__

__Data Field__

__Details__

__Eligibility Criteria__

Registered SME, turnover LKR 20M \- 1,000M, 1 \- 2 years operation

__Loan Amount__

LKR 500,000 \- 50,000,000

__Interest Rate__

8 \- 12% \(Govt schemes\), 12 \- 16% \(Commercial\)

__Collateral Requirements__

Property, machinery, business assets, personal guarantee

__Repayment Period__

3\- 5 years \(0 \- 6 months grace\)

__Key Benefits__

Concessionary rates, flexible collateral, vendor payments

__Loan Purpose__

Expansion, working capital, machinery, construction

__Documents Required__

Application, BR, financials, bank statements, collateral docs

__Approval Timeline__

10 \- 14 days

\(Commercial Bank of Ceylon, n\.d\.\)

## <a id="_Toc220821806"></a>__Bank of Ceylon \(BOC\) – SMILE III / SME Loans__

__Data Field__

__Details__

__Eligibility Criteria__

MSME manufacturing, assets < LKR 150M, 25% equity

__Loan Amount__

Up to LKR 25,000,000

__Interest Rate__

5 \- 7%

__Collateral Requirements__

Bank discretion may be waived for small loans

__Repayment Period__

Up to 10 years \(\+ 24 months grace\)

__Key Benefits__

Very low rates, long tenure, rural priority

__Loan Purpose__

Machinery, factory upgrades, working capital

__Documents Required__

Application, BR, project proposal, financials

__Approval Timeline__

14 \- 21 days

\(Bank of Ceylon \(BOC\) , n\.d\.\)
