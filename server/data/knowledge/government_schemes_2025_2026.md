# Sri Lankan Government MSME Loan Schemes (2025-2026)

## RE-MSME PLUS Loan Scheme (2026)
- **Status**: New scheme integrating SMILE III, E-FRIENDS II, and original RE-MSME.
- **Allocation**: Rs. 25,000 million for 2026.
- **Eligibility**: Agriculture, manufacturing, tourism, export-oriented, services, and startups.
- **Loan Limits**:
  - Micro: Up to Rs. 250,000 (Working Capital).
  - Small: Up to Rs. 1 million.
  - Medium/Large: Up to Rs. 25 million.
- **Interest Rate**: 3% per annum (concessional).
- **Repayment**: 3 years with a 6-month grace period.

## National Credit Guarantee Institution (NCGI)
- **Goal for 2026**: Issue Rs. 7,000 million in credit guarantees.
- **Target**: Facilitate ~Rs. 10,000 million in MSME lending.
- **Context**: Helps businesses without sufficient collateral to access bank loans.

## RE-MSME Disaster Relief Loan Scheme
- **Purpose**: Assist MSMEs affected by disasters.
- **Interest Rate**: 3% per annum.
- **Term**: 3 years with 6-month grace period.
- **Limits**: Rs. 250,000 (Micro), Rs. 1,000,000 (Small/Medium).

## CBSL Relief Measures (2025)
- **Eligibility**: SMEs classified as non-performing (Stage 3) on/after April 1, 2019.
- **Rescheduling Deadline**: Applications by March 31, 2025; finalization by June 30, 2025.
- **Repayment Commencement**:
  - Loans < Rs. 25M: Dec 31, 2025.
  - Loans Rs. 25M - 50M: Sep 30, 2025.
  - Loans > Rs. 50M: June 30, 2025.
- **Interest Waivers**: Up to 65% for certain loan tiers if settled within specified periods.
- **Parate Execution Suspension**: In effect until March 31, 2025.

## Disaster-Related Relief (Cyclone Ditwah - Late 2025)
- **Request Deadline**: January 15, 2026.
- **Relief**: 3-6 month suspension of capital/interest repayments.
- **New Loans**: Max 9% fixed interest for up to 2 years.

## Budget 2026 Highlights
- Lowering investment thresholds for tax incentives.
- Consolidating SME support under the Industrial Development Board.
- New schemes: SME Development Loan Scheme, Agricultural Value Chain Development.
