# National Enterprise Development Authority (NEDA) SME Support

## Regional Enterprise Development Program
- NEDA implements short-term capacity development training, financial assistance, and market facilitation.
- Available in all districts at the regional level.

## Cluster/Sector Development Program
- Cost-effective Business Development Services (BDS) provided to whole clusters of enterprises rather than individual ones.
- Focuses on specific sectors to maximize the impact of limited funds.

## Enterprise Forum
- Creates partnerships between local private and public sector stakeholders.
- Realizes locality's economic potential through stakeholder engagement.

## Key Areas of Expertise
- **Market Facilitation**: Exploiting untapped markets.
- **Techno-Entrepreneurship**: Promoting technology-based startups and business incubation.
- **Access to Finance**: Supporting findings for appropriate financial solutions.
- **Policy Support**: Platform to lobby issues and derive solutions.
- **Capacity Development**: Skills, attitude, and knowledge training.

## Recent Initiatives (2024)
- **UPLIFT Project**: Partnered with ILO for capacity-building for MSMEs, focusing on migrant returnees and vulnerable communities.
- **GET Ahead Training**: Collaboration with ILO for "Gender and Enterprise Together" training of trainers.
- **Youth Entrepreneurship**: Skill development program for vocational training students.
